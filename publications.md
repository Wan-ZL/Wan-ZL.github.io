My research spans deep reinforcement learning, uncertainty quantification, game-theoretic decision making, and cybersecurity, and it is the foundation of the LLM post-training and evaluation work I do now. In total I have 15 papers (9 first-author), with 4 under review. A selection is below, and the full list is on **[Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=0Z8N7zYAAAAJ)**. An asterisk (*) marks equal contribution.
